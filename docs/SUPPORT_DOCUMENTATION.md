## GETTING STARTED

### First Time User? Start Here!

**What is Biography Writer?**
Biography Writer is an AI-powered skill that guides you through creating a professionally written biography. It works by:
1. Asking structured questions about your life (or someone else's)
2. Listening to your answers
3. Writing beautiful, flowing narrative text
4. Organizing it into a professional Word document
5. Delivering a ready-to-use biography

**How Long Does It Take?**
- Interview: 30-45 minutes (depending on detail level)
- Document Generation: 2-5 minutes (automatic)
- **Total Time**: About 1 hour from start to finish

**What Do I Need?**
- Access to Claude (Chat, App, or Desktop)
- 15-20 minutes of quiet time to focus
- Optional: Photos (JPG or PNG format)
- That's it!

### Quick Start (5 Steps)

**Step 1: Start the Skill**
```
You: "I want to create a biography"
or
"Use Biography Writer skill"
```

**Step 2: Provide Basic Info**
```
Skill: "Who are we creating this biography for?"
You: "John Doe" (or your name)
```

**Step 3: Go Through Interview**
```
Skill asks ~30 questions across 6 life stages
You answer each question (detailed or brief)
Takes about 30-45 minutes
```

**Step 4: Add Photos (Optional)**
```
Skill: "Would you like to add photos?"
You: Upload 1-2 photos per stage (optional)
```

**Step 5: Get Your Biography**
```
Skill generates Word document
Download: biography_john_doe.docx
Ready to use immediately!
```

## FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

### Account & Access

**Q: Is Biography Writer free?**
A: Yes! Biography Writer is completely free to use. No hidden charges, no subscription required.

**Q: Do I need a special account?**
A: No. If you have access to Claude, you have access to Biography Writer. Just start using it.

**Q: Can multiple people use the same account?**
A: Yes, but each biography needs its own conversation/session.

**Q: Is my data saved?**
A: Your final biography is saved as a Word file that you download. Intermediate interview data is not stored unless you save it.

### Using the Skill

**Q: Can I pause and come back later?**
A: Yes! If you need to stop mid-interview, you can save your progress. When you return, you can resume or start fresh.

**Q: What if I forget to answer a question?**
A: You can always go back and add missing information. Your Word document can be edited afterward.

**Q: Can I create multiple biographies?**
A: Yes! Start a new conversation for each person you want to write about.

**Q: What if the biography doesn't sound right?**
A: You can edit the Word document after generation. Biography Writer creates a solid foundation that you can refine.

**Q: Can I make the biography longer or shorter?**
A: Yes! Edit the Word document to add more content or remove sections.

**Q: What languages does it support?**
A: Currently English and Indonesian. Other languages coming in v1.1.

### Photos & Images

**Q: What photo formats work?**
A: JPG and PNG files work best. Size should be under 5MB per photo.

**Q: How many photos can I add?**
A: 1-2 photos per life stage recommended. More can slow down document generation.

**Q: What if I don't have good photos?**
A: Photos are completely optional! Your biography works perfectly without them.

**Q: Can I add photos after generation?**
A: Yes! Open the Word document and insert photos manually if needed.

### Output & Sharing

**Q: What format is the output?**
A: Standard Microsoft Word format (.docx). Works with Word, Google Docs, LibreOffice, and all major word processors.

**Q: Can I print my biography?**
A: Yes! The document is fully formatted and ready for professional printing.

**Q: Can I convert to PDF?**
A: Yes! Open in Word and "Save As" PDF, or use any PDF conversion tool.

**Q: Can I share my biography?**
A: Yes! Email the file, upload to cloud storage, or print physical copies.

**Q: Is the document editable?**
A: Yes! You have full editing rights. Modify text, formatting, and structure however you like.

### Data & Privacy

**Q: Where is my data stored?**
A: Your biography is stored locally in the Word file you download. We don't keep copies on our servers.

**Q: Is my data private?**
A: Yes. Your biographical information is private and only you have access to it.

**Q: Can Biography Writer access my personal information?**
A: Only information you explicitly share during the interview. No access to your email, contacts, or other data.

**Q: What happens to my data if Biography Writer is discontinued?**
A: Your Word documents are yours to keep. You'll always have your biographies even if the service changes.

### Technical Questions

**Q: What are the system requirements?**
A: Any device with Claude access (browser, desktop app, or mobile app). No special software needed.

**Q: Does it work offline?**
A: Interview works online. You can edit the Word document offline afterward.

**Q: What's the maximum length for a biography?**
A: No hard limit! You can have as many chapters and pages as needed.

**Q: Is there a file size limit?**
A: Typical biographies are 20-50 KB. Very photo-heavy documents might be 100+ KB.

## TROUBLESHOOTING GUIDE

### Common Issues & Solutions

#### Issue 1: Interview Questions Seem Repetitive

**Problem**: Some questions feel similar or redundant.

**Solution**:
- Different questions are designed to capture different perspectives
- It's okay to give brief answers if you already covered something
- The skill uses different angles to build a complete picture

**Prevention**: Understand that slight repetition helps create depth and nuance in your biography.

#### Issue 2: Generated Biography Doesn't Match My Style

**Problem**: The biography sounds too formal or not formal enough.

**Solution**:
- Biography Writer uses semi-formal tone which works for most readers
- You can edit the Word document to change the tone:
  - Make it more casual: Replace "furthermore" with "also"
  - Make it more formal: Expand abbreviations, use fuller sentences
- Use Word's find/replace feature for bulk changes

**Prevention**: Provide specific tone guidance in your interview answers if you have preferences.

#### Issue 3: Photos Not Appearing in Document

**Problem**: I uploaded photos but they're not in the Word document.

**Solutions**:
1. **Photo file issues**:
   - Make sure file is JPG or PNG
   - File size under 5MB
   - Clear file name (avoid special characters)

2. **Browser/connection issues**:
   - Refresh the page and try again
   - Use Chrome, Firefox, or Safari
   - Check internet connection

3. **Manual insertion**:
   - Open the generated Word document
   - Use Word's "Insert > Pictures" to add photos manually
   - Place them where you want

**Prevention**: Test photo upload with a small file first before uploading large batches.

#### Issue 4: Document Won't Open

**Problem**: The downloaded .docx file won't open in Word or Google Docs.

**Solutions**:
1. **File corruption**:
   - Try downloading again
   - Browser might have corrupted the download
   
2. **Software issue**:
   - Use Google Docs (works in browser)
   - Try LibreOffice Writer (free)
   - Update your Word version

3. **File permissions**:
   - Check that the file isn't read-only
   - Try saving with a different name

**Prevention**: Use Chrome for downloads. Google Docs works universally.

#### Issue 5: Interview Keeps Getting Disconnected

**Problem**: Mid-interview, the connection drops or session ends.

**Solutions**:
1. **Internet connection**:
   - Check your wifi/connection
   - Switch to wired if possible
   - Move closer to router

2. **Browser issues**:
   - Clear browser cache
   - Try different browser
   - Disable extensions temporarily

3. **Session timeout**:
   - Biography Writer remembers your answers
   - You can resume where you left off
   - Session typically saved every 5 minutes

**Prevention**: Make sure you have stable internet before starting. Avoid using VPNs that might cause disconnections.

#### Issue 6: Generated Text Has Typos or Grammar Errors

**Problem**: The biography has occasional spelling or grammar mistakes.

**Solutions**:
1. **Quick fix**:
   - Open in Word
   - Use spell-check (F7 in Word)
   - Correct any flagged items

2. **Manual editing**:
   - Read through and fix as you go
   - Use Grammarly or similar tool

3. **Quality feedback**:
   - Report the error to support
   - Helps us improve the system

**Prevention**: Provide clear, well-written answers. Biography Writer's quality improves with better input.

## BEST PRACTICES

### For Better Results

**1. Take Your Time with Answers**
```
❌ Bad: "Born 1970"
✅ Good: "Born January 15, 1970 in Jakarta to parents who valued education"
```
More detail = better biography!

**2. Include Specific Examples**
```
❌ Bad: "Was good at school"
✅ Good: "Placed first in state science competition in 1985 and was invited to represent the state nationally"
```
Stories make biographies come alive!

**3. Mention Influential People**
```
❌ Bad: "Had good teachers"
✅ Good: "Professor Hartono changed my perspective on engineering and mentored me through my thesis"
```
Named people add authenticity!

**4. Connect the Dots**
```
❌ Bad: "Studied engineering. Worked in finance. Started charity."
✅ Good: "My engineering background taught me to think systematically, which I applied to finance, then used that success to fund my childhood education charity"
```
Connections create narrative flow!

**5. Include Emotions & Reactions**
```
❌ Bad: "Got promoted"
✅ Good: "The promotion was overwhelming and humbling - I realized my team's hard work was being recognized"
```
Emotions make biographies meaningful!

### Photo Best Practices

**File Format**
- ✅ Use JPG or PNG
- ❌ Avoid BMP, GIF, or TIFF

**File Size**
- ✅ Under 5MB per photo
- ✅ Compress large photos
- ❌ No photos over 10MB

**Image Quality**
- ✅ Clear, well-lit photos
- ✅ Historical significance
- ✅ Recent professional photos
- ❌ Blurry or pixelated images

**Photo Selection**
- ✅ 1-2 photos per life stage
- ✅ Photos that represent key moments
- ✅ Mix of formal and candid
- ❌ Dozens of similar photos

### Document Organization

**After Generation**
1. Save to Cloud (Google Drive, Dropbox)
2. Create backups
3. Share with family for feedback
4. Edit and refine
5. Print or distribute final version

**Sharing Tips**
- Share with spouse/partner for fact-checking
- Get family feedback before finalizing
- Save multiple versions if making major edits
- Use track changes when collaborating

## TIPS & TRICKS

### Pro Tips for Better Biographies

**1. Keep a Notepad Nearby**
Before starting, jot down key dates, names, and events. Having them ready speeds up the process.

**2. Use the Editing Power**
Don't stress about perfection during interview. The Word document is fully editable. Fix anything afterward.

**3. Get Feedback**
Share draft with family/friends. Their input often adds valuable context or corrects details.

**4. Add Transitions**
Use Word to add connecting sentences between paragraphs if they feel disjointed.

**5. Include Statistics**
"Managed $5M budget" is more impactful than "managed large budget."

**6. Tell Stories**
Specific anecdotes are more engaging than general statements.

**7. Theme It**
Add your color scheme or custom formatting to make it uniquely yours.

**8. Create Multiple Versions**
- Professional version for LinkedIn
- Family version with personal details
- Legacy version for archives

### Customization Ideas

**Professional Biography**
- Focus on career achievements
- Emphasize education and credentials
- Include professional accomplishments

**Family History Version**
- Include family tree references
- Add personal relationship details
- Include beloved memories

**Legacy Document**
- Focus on life lessons learned
- Emphasize values and principles
- Include advice for future generations
