## USER ONBOARDING GUIDE

### Getting Started (5 Minutes)

**Step 1: Start the Skill**
- Open Claude or Biography Writer app
- Say: "I want to use Biography Writer"
- Skill loads and introduces itself

**Step 2: Provide Your Name**
- Skill asks: "Who's biography are we creating?"
- You provide the person's full name
- Skill confirms and proceeds

**Step 3: Interview Process** (4 life stages, ~30 minutes)
- **Stage 1: Masa Kecil** - Early life questions (5 min)
- **Stage 2: Pendidikan** - Education questions (5 min)
- **Stage 3: Karir Awal** - Career questions (5 min)
- **Stage 4: Pencapaian Utama** - Achievements questions (5 min)
- **Stage 5: Kehidupan Keluarga** - Family questions (5 min)
- **Stage 6: Warisan** - Legacy questions (5 min)

**Step 4: Photo Upload** (Optional, 5 minutes)
- Skill offers to add photos
- You can upload 1-2 photos per stage
- Skill adds captions automatically

**Step 5: Generate Document**
- Skill processes all information
- Creates Word document
- Delivers to you ready to use

**Step 6: Download & Share**
- Download the .docx file
- Open in Word, Google Docs, or any word processor
- Share with family, friends, or print

## FAQ FOR LAUNCH

### Q: How long does it take?
A: The interview takes about 30-45 minutes depending on how detailed you want to be. Document generation is automatic and fast.

### Q: What if I don't have much information?
A: Biography Writer works with any amount of information - minimal or extensive. The skill adapts to what you provide.

### Q: Can I edit the biography after?
A: Yes! The document is a standard Word file that you can edit, add to, or modify as much as you like.

### Q: Can I add more photos?
A: Yes, you can add photos during the interview, and you can also manually insert them into the Word document afterward.

### Q: What format is the output?
A: Biography Writer generates a .docx file (Word format) that works with Microsoft Word, Google Docs, LibreOffice, and all major word processors.

### Q: Is my data private?
A: Yes. Your biographical data is processed securely and only you have access to your final document.

### Q: Can I create multiple biographies?
A: Absolutely! You can create one biography per person/conversation. Start a new conversation for each person.

### Q: Does it cost anything?
A: No! Biography Writer is completely free to use.

### Q: Can I customize the life stages?
A: In v1.0, the 6 life stages are fixed. We're planning customizable stages for v1.1.

### Q: What if I make mistakes in my answers?
A: You can always edit the Word document afterward. You can also provide additional information if you remember something later.

### Q: Can I share my biography?
A: Yes! The Word document can be easily shared, printed, or distributed to family and friends.

### Q: What are the system requirements?
A: Biography Writer works on any device with access to Claude. Documents are created as standard Word files compatible with all platforms.
